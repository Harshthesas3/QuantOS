# QuantOS Licensing Guide

QuantOS is available under two licensing tracks:

- **Community Edition** for personal learning, students, educators, research, and other non-commercial use
- **Commercial Edition** for organizations and any commercial use

## Community Edition

The Community Edition is free to use when your use case is clearly non-commercial.

You may use it for:

- Personal learning
- Student coursework and self-study
- Classroom instruction and educational demonstrations
- Academic research
- Internal non-commercial experimentation

You may not use the Community Edition for commercial activity, redistribution, bundling, or deployment at scale by an organization that is not covered by a commercial license.

## Commercial Edition

The Commercial Edition is required when QuantOS is used in a commercial context or in a deployment that goes beyond the Community Edition permissions.

Commercial terms are negotiated separately and may include:

- Deployment rights
- Redistribution rights
- Embedding rights
- Support
- Maintenance updates
- Security patches

## Who Needs a Commercial License?

You likely need a commercial license if you are:

- A company using QuantOS internally for business operations
- An organization deploying QuantOS to employees, customers, or clients
- A university deploying QuantOS at scale beyond personal or classroom use
- A training company or paid education provider
- A distributor, reseller, or integrator
- A product team bundling QuantOS into another application or service

When in doubt, contact the QuantOS team before shipping or distributing the software.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is QuantOS open source?

QuantOS is source-available under a dual-licensing model. The Community Edition is free for defined non-commercial uses, while commercial uses require a separate license.

### Can I use QuantOS in my classroom?

Yes, if the use is educational and non-commercial.

### Can I use QuantOS in a company research lab?

Not without a commercial license if the lab is part of a commercial organization or the use supports commercial work.

### Can I modify QuantOS?

Yes, for permitted Community Edition use or under the terms of a commercial license.

### Can I redistribute QuantOS?

Only if the redistribution is allowed by the applicable license. Redistribution for commercial purposes requires a commercial license.

## How to Obtain a Commercial License

1. Review your intended use and confirm that the Community Edition does not apply.
2. Contact the QuantOS team at commercial@quantos.dev.
3. Describe your use case, deployment size, redistribution plans, and support requirements.
4. Review the proposed commercial terms and ensure legal review before signing.
